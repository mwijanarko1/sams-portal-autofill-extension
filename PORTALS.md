## CRM sources

Copy reads two approved sources: the SAMS CRM at `https://app.samscrm.co.uk` (lead Profile and Applications tabs) and Campora APPLY at `https://v-sams-backend.samsapply.co.uk` (application route `/applyuser/show/<id>/profile`). Campora's read-only profile loads when its Profile tab is opened; the reader opens that tab, maps only the canonical fields the engine and portal maps consume, and restores the previous tab. Reference records, status flags, student quality and the level badge are not mapped. Campora documents are not listed or fetched; staff attach them manually. A first real Campora-to-Bangor no-save sample passed on 2026-10-25: Copy returned 30 fields and Bangor education, employment and finance filled eight controls, with existing/blocked values preserved, zero blocked writes and the cache cleared. This is one application and one portal, not full acceptance; draft ownership was not verified, so treat it as a no-save field-fill smoke test. The full Campora map, deliberate omissions and read-only section endpoints are in [docs/history/CAMPORA.md](docs/history/CAMPORA.md).
