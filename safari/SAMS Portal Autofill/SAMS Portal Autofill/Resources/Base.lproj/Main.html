SAMS Portal Autofill

Safari extension for SAMS Global staff

You can turn on SAMS Portal Autofill’s extension in Safari Extensions preferences.

SAMS Portal Autofill’s extension is currently on. You can turn it off in Safari Extensions preferences.

SAMS Portal Autofill’s extension is currently off. You can turn it on in Safari Extensions preferences.

  1. Turn on SAMS Portal Autofill in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.
  2. Allow it to access only the approved SAMS CRM and university portal websites when Safari asks.
  3. Use the SAMS Portal Autofill toolbar button in Safari to copy a student profile and fill a portal draft.

This app only helps you turn on the Safari extension. It never receives or stores student data.
